About Gmail

Gmail personal accounts cannot be used as the sending address directly. However you can set your Gmail as the Reply-to address above — clients will reply straight to your Gmail inbox.

For sending, Resend requires a verified domain. If you own a domain, verify it on resend.com/domains and use that as your From address (e.g. info@yourbusiness.com).
